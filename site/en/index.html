FAQ

Common questions

Pick a question — Ilana answers.

All answers
How long does the graduated procedure take?
Usually several years: status is granted in stages and renewed. Exact timing depends on your case — we’ll go over it in the consultation.
Do we have to be married?
No. Common-law partners can go through the procedure too, but they need to prove a shared life.
The Ministry refused. What now?
A refusal can be appealed. Don’t wait — appeals have deadlines. Send me the decision and I’ll tell you your options.
What is the interview like?
Partners are usually interviewed separately about their life together. Preparation helps you answer calmly and accurately.
How much does a consultation cost?
The first consultation is free. After that, the fee depends on the complexity of the case — I’ll tell you in advance.
Can we talk in Russian or English?
Yes. I work in Hebrew, Russian and English.
